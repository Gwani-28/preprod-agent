# Roadmap

Preproduction Agent is actively maintained. Priorities may shift with feedback
and contributions.

Have an idea? Open a [feature request](https://github.com/Gwani-28/preprod-agent/issues/new/choose).

## Near term

- **Project import/export (JSON)** — back up a project or move it between
  devices/browsers (currently data lives only in one browser's `localStorage`).
- **Format presets** — starter templates and checklists tuned for short film /
  commercial / music video.
- **Export polish** — nicer PPM Book PDF/HTML layout and print styling.

## Mid term

- **English UI localization** (the app ships Korean today).
- **Visual board improvements** — drag-and-drop ordering, image captions.
- **Per-day call-sheet export** from the shooting plan.

## Long term

- **Optional sharing** — export a read-only PPM Book link without introducing a
  mandatory backend.
- **Accessibility** pass (keyboard navigation, screen readers).

## Non-goals

- Mandatory accounts, cloud lock-in, or telemetry. The app stays local-first.
